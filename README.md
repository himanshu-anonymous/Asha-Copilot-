## 💎 The Vision (Sapphire Corp)
Developed by **Himanshu Patil**, ASHA Copilot is a decentralized, clinical-grade Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) designed for "Shadow Zones"—rural areas with zero internet and limited medical professional density. 

Our mission is to empower frontline health workers (ASHAs) with Generative UI diagnostics and a "Vahak-Setu" (Vehicle Bridge) data relay system.

---

## 🛠️ Core Technical Pillars

### 1. Generative UI Diagnostics (Tambo SDK)
Replaces complex, fragmented forms with AI-driven screening. The assistant parses natural language to render structured **DiagnosticCards** instantly.

### 2. The Offline Vault (Dexie.js)
Ensures 100% data integrity in regions with inadequate infrastructure. All patient records are "vaulted" locally in a structured IndexedDB until a sync opportunity arises.

### 3. Vahak-Setu Mesh Sync
A revolutionary peer-to-peer relay system. It turns rural vehicles (Vahaks) into data carriers, syncing vaulted records to the city hospital via a decentralized Hydra-Mesh network.

### 4. Neural Logistics (ST-GNN)
Uses Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks to predict road reliability during monsoons. Workers receive real-time **RouteAlerts** to bypass infrastructure washouts.

### 5. Inclusivity Bridge (Bhashini API)
Overcomes language barriers by providing neural translations into local dialects (Hindi, Marathi, etc.), ensuring clinical instructions are understood by all.
